# Prognosis_plus_Disease_Prediction_Using_Ensemble_Learning

Project Demo Link: https://drive.google.com/file/d/1X0LYiq-xV0AZz44Q7PvGQ9itkUeK8aZ4/view?usp=sharing

The scope of our project, centered around disease prediction through Ensemble 
Learning, extends across a diverse spectrum of health conditions. 

By integrating three robust algorithms – 
Decision Tree, Random Forest, and Gradient Boosting – through a unified Voting Classifier, 
we aim to enhance the accuracy and reliability of our predictive model.

Beyond the realm of early disease detection, our project holds the potential to 
revolutionize healthcare by providing valuable insights for personalized treatment 
plans. Leveraging artificial intelligence to analyze individual patient data enables us 
to predict the most effective treatments, contributing to superior patient outcomes 
and potentially reducing healthcare costs.

While emphasizing the transformative impact on disease diagnosis and treatment, 
we are committed to upholding ethical standards and implementing stringent data 
privacy measures.
